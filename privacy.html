Privacy Policy

Ananke rclone is a personal backup utility operated by a single individual for his own Google account. It has no other users.

What it accesses

The application requests read-only access to Google Drive (drive.readonly). It reads file contents and metadata in order to copy them to private local storage.

What it does with data

Data is copied to private storage under the sole control of the account owner. Nothing is transmitted to any third party, shared, sold, or used for any purpose other than backup. No analytics or tracking of any kind are performed.

Retention and deletion

Copied data is retained on private storage at the account owner's discretion and may be deleted at any time. Authorization may be revoked at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
